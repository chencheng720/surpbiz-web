The Lifecycle Model

Map the ground.

Before ground is broken, we map the terrain. Aerial geophysics, remote sensing, and geochemical surveys — the foundational data that supports project planning and decision-making.

  • Aerial geophysical surveys
  • Remote sensing & satellite data
  • Geochemical sampling
  • Resource estimation

Build and supply.

We supply the heavy equipment and on-site services that turn project plans into productive operations — from excavation to material handling.

  • Heavy equipment supply
  • Excavators & dump trucks

  • Equipment rental
  • Site security systems
  • Logistics & camp setup
